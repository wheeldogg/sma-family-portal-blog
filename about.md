**SMA Family Portal** is a blog for families affected by Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA) in Ireland.

We share research updates, congress reports, practical tips, and lived experience from the Irish SMA community. Our goal is to help families stay informed, connected, and empowered as the treatment landscape evolves.

This site is maintained by Shane Whelan — a software engineer and father of Theo, who has SMA Type 1.

If you'd like to get in touch, reach out through [SMA Ireland](https://www.smaireland.com/).
